Our Story

Born from a gap in Pakistan.

FUDG was founded by Eesha Muzammil, a pastry chef who has spent years working with chocolate and trained at Le Cordon Bleu. Somewhere along the way, she noticed something missing in Pakistan's market. Nobody was making truly good brownies commercially.

A good brownie isn't just a dessert. It's a whole experience.

So we started experimenting. Day and night. Trial after trial. Dozens of batches. Countless recipe tweaks. All chasing one thing: a brownie that finally felt right.

Our goal was simple. Bring Islamabad and Rawalpindi the real brownies they had never tasted before, and eventually, all of Pakistan.

For the first time in Pakistan, FUDG is bringing you the best brownies this country has ever had.
